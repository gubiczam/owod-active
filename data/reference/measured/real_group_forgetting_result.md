## The frozen grouping, and how thin it is on the old classes

The head/medium/tail assignment is `data/detector_class_groups.csv`, frozen before any of these runs. Applied to the 19 task-1 classes it splits them **11 / 7 / 1**.

That last number is the honest limit of this table: the old-class tail band contains **1 class**. Its "group mAP" is one class's AP50, and it is labelled as such everywhere below. The 21 task-2 classes split 8 / 3 / 10, so the *new*-class tail band is the one with enough members to carry a group claim.

## What is held equal, and what is not

Equal by construction: annotated regions, starting weights, epochs, learning rate, batch size, evaluator, and the absence of replay. **Not** equal: unique images selected, images kept for training, gradient steps (= kept images, since epochs=1 and batch=1), and t2 supervision boxes. PROB trains on every t2 box in a selected image, not only on the queried regions, so the supervision the training receives is not proportional to what the acquisition discovered.
